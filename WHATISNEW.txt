XADON AI — Update pack (2026-11-25)
====================================
1) .vv FIXED ("media key not found")
   - New shared engine src/lib/viewonce.js: tries full raw from rawstore first,
     unwraps viewOnceMessageV2/ephemeral wrappers, retries with reupload.
   - .vv, .vvdl, .vvp all use it. Tip: reply .vv BEFORE opening the view-once;
     expired/already-opened media cannot be recovered.

2) NEW .autostatuslike (owner)
   - .autostatuslike on | off  (toggle, likes with ❤️ by default)
   - .autostatuslike 💚        (sets emoji + turns ON)
   - Aliases: statuslike, autolike, likestatus
   - Wired in src/connection.js: every incoming status@broadcast is viewed,
     and when armed it is liked with your emoji.

3) .play FIXED (multi-API fallback chain)
   - yt-search for lookup, then tries 6 audio endpoints in order
     (hector worker, davidcyril x2, ryzen, dreaded, widipe) + cobalt fallback.
   - Accepts song name OR direct YouTube link. Buffers bytes if URL send fails.

4) .hidetag refreshed + NEW .hidetagall
   - .hidetag [text] (reply to media keeps media): silent tag in current group.
   - .hidetagall (reply to text/media) [caption] — OWNER ONLY:
     forwards that message to EVERY group with silent per-group mentions,
     5s gap between groups to prevent spam/bans.
     Aliases: htagall, silenttagall, broadcasttag.

5) .gstatusall NOW SUPPORTS MEDIA
   - Reply to image/video/audio (+ optional caption, --bg=color supported)
     with .gstatusall to broadcast it as group-status to all groups.
   - Plain-text mode unchanged.

6) NEW .jidtag (CODY-style group-JID tag)
   - .jidtag how are you  → sends "@how are you" with the GROUP JID as the
     mention, rendering one @pill with your custom text.
   - Aliases: gjidtag, groupjidtag, tagjid. Group + admin only.
